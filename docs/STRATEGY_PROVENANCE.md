# Strategy provenance

The included strategy specifications implement common research patterns and
record bibliographic provenance without redistributing source text.

Reference categories include:

- cross-sectional momentum, residual momentum, low-volatility, channel, and
  mean-reversion research;
- systematic trend following, volatility scaling, and EWMAC-style rules;
- moving-average, breakout, channel, and volatility-system design; and
- portfolio construction, active management, and alpha-research methodology.

Representative published references include work by Zura Kakushadze and Willie
Yu; Robert Carver; Perry Kaufman; Andreas Clenow; Igor Tulchinsky and
contributors; and Richard Grinold and Ronald Kahn. Users are responsible for
obtaining source materials through publishers, libraries, official open-access
repositories, or other lawful channels.

No book text, PDF, OCR output, or commercial dataset is included in this public
repository. Citations and provenance metadata are not a substitute for
independent implementation review or empirical validation.
