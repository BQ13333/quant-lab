## Advice Not Adopted

The panel suggested many alternative lookbacks, thresholds, signs, winsor levels, window shifts, and model families. Those are not repairs: after results, they would be hidden optimization. They may be considered only as separately preregistered future hypotheses.

Rough paths, Hawkes processes, HJB methods, neural models, generative models, and adaptive learning are also unnecessary for this wave. The correct scientific test is whether four simple source-backed hypotheses survive conservative, fixed validation. Complexity is not evidence.
