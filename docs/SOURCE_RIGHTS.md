# Source rights

20 November 2026, Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Decided by Benjamin Quek at 18:26.

Fundamentals for this programme come from official public filings only:

| Market | Source | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bursa Malaysia | [Bursa company announcements](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market_information/announcements/company_announcement) | Issuer announcements and financial reports published by the exchange |
| Hong Kong | [HKEXnews](https://www.hkexnews.hk/) | Issuer announcements, circulars, and reports |
| China A-shares | [CNINFO](https://www.cninfo.com.cn/) | Public disclosure site for Shanghai and Shenzhen filings. Beijing Stock Exchange records stay out |

Use is subject to access rights. A page that loads, a robots rule, or a successful download is not a licence to crawl, redistribute, or run an unattended bulk pull. Bursa's information-services guidelines describe licensed distribution. Permission for the proposed automated use is not established. A 403, a timeout, or a denial stops that source. It is not a prompt to rotate addresses or solve a challenge.

Out of bounds for fundamentals:

- Paid feeds and paid consensus histories, including a new Refinitiv, LSEG, Bloomberg, FactSet, Wind, or similar pull.
- AkShare, BaoStock, Stooq, and Yahoo as fundamental evidence. Yahoo remains a smoke path for prices where an existing adapter already has it. It does not establish a filing date.
- Any feed that has to be bought. The 16 November no-spend decision still stands.

The private `china_lake` already holds prices, a point-in-time master, and an HK vertical. Its vendor provenance is not re-labelled as an official filing by this note. Unknown provenance stays quarantined for cloud admission. This repository does not copy that lake.

Prices are a separate problem from filings. Official filings do not by themselves repair a missing survivorship-aware daily price panel. No paid price feed is added here.
